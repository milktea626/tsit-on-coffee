配送常見問題Common Delivery Questions

本地送貨最低訂購金額是多少？What is the minimum for free local delivery?

批發夥伴達到指定訂購金額即可享免費本地配送。詳情請聯絡我們的銷售代表。Wholesale partners enjoy free local delivery upon reaching the minimum order value. Please contact our sales representative for details.

可否指定送貨時間？Can I specify a delivery time?

可以，我們會盡力配合您的營業時間安排送貨，請在下訂時註明您的優先送貨時段。Yes, we will do our best to accommodate your business hours. Please indicate your preferred delivery window when placing your order.

海外郵寄如何計算運費？How is international shipping calculated?

國際郵費按重量及目的地計算，請聯絡我們獲取準確報價。International shipping is calculated by weight and destination. Please contact us for an accurate quote.

離島可以送貨嗎？Do you deliver to outlying islands?

可以，我們覆蓋大嶼山、南丫島、長洲等主要離島，惟送貨排程可能有所不同，請提前聯絡安排。Yes, we cover major outlying islands including Lantau, Lamma and Cheung Chau. Schedules may differ — please contact us in advance to arrange.

本地或海外，我們都能為您安排最合適的送貨方案。Local or international — we'll find the best delivery solution for you.
