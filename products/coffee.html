中度烘焙 ★ 最受歡迎Medium Roast ★ Most Popular

均衡的酸甜苦味，帶有堅果及焦糖香氣，是香港茶餐廳最常用的咖啡烘焙程度。Balanced acidity, sweetness and bitterness with nutty caramel notes — the most popular roast for HK cha chaan teng.

適合：港式咖啡、EspressoBest for: HK Coffee, Espresso
🌑

深度烘焙Dark Roast

濃郁苦香，帶有可可及烤焦糖風味，適合偏好濃味咖啡的香港食客及傳統茶餐廳。Bold and smoky with cocoa and dark caramel — perfect for customers who love strong Hong Kong-style coffee.
