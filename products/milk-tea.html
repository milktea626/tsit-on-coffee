港式奶茶文化Hong Kong Milk Tea Culture

絲襪奶茶：香港的飲食文化遺產Silk Stocking Milk Tea: Hong Kong's Culinary Heritage

港式奶茶（絲襪奶茶）是香港獨有的飲品文化，以多種錫蘭紅茶混合，經反覆拉茶而成，口感絲滑濃郁，與眾不同。 Hong Kong milk tea (silk stocking tea) is a unique cultural institution — blended from multiple Ceylon teas and repeatedly "pulled" for a silky, full-bodied texture unlike any other.

捷安咖啡自1978年起鑽研港式奶茶茶葉配方，以超過45年的調配經驗，深諳不同茶葉的比例及特性，為您調配出最地道的香港奶茶味道。 Tsit On Coffee has been perfecting HK milk tea blends since 1978 — over 45 years of expertise in the precise ratios and characteristics of each tea variety to create the most authentic Hong Kong milk tea.
