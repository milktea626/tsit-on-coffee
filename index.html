捷安咖啡的故事Our Story

根植香港，傳承超過半世紀 Rooted in Hong Kong, Trusted for Over 55 Years

1970年，捷安咖啡在香港成立，憑著對咖啡品質的堅持，由一家小型烘焙工廠發展成為全港最具規模的咖啡批發商之一。 Founded in 1970, Tsit On Coffee began as a small roasting workshop. Over 55 years, our unwavering commitment to quality has made us one of Hong Kong's most trusted coffee wholesale partners.

我們服務涵蓋咖啡批發、港式奶茶茶葉、茶餐廳乾糧及罐頭食品，配合自家物流車隊，全面支持香港各區餐飲業。 Our services span coffee wholesale, HK milk tea blends, cha chaan teng dry and canned goods, backed by our own logistics fleet — a true one-stop F&B partner.
