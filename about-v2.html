關於我們About Us

植根香港 超過55年Rooted in Hong Kong Since 1970

自1970年起服務香港餐飲業，以自家烘焙工廠及專業服務贏得業界信賴。Serving Hong Kong's food and beverage industry since 1970 with our own roasting facility and professional service.

我們的故事Our Story

超過半世紀的堅持Over Half a Century of Dedication

捷安咖啡有限公司成立於1970年，是香港歷史悠久的咖啡及茶餐廳食材批發商。超過55年來，我們專注為香港餐飲業提供優質咖啡豆、港式奶茶茶葉及各類餐飲食材，服務遍及全港茶餐廳、快餐店、酒樓及餐廳。Tsit On Coffee Ltd was established in 1970 as one of Hong Kong's longest-standing coffee and cha chaan teng ingredient wholesalers. For over 55 years, we have specialized in providing premium coffee beans, Hong Kong-style milk tea blends, and various food and beverage ingredients to restaurants across Hong Kong — serving cha chaan tengs, fast food outlets, Chinese restaurants, and cafés throughout all 18 districts.

我們以自家烘焙工廠為核心優勢，從生豆採購到烘焙出貨全程掌控品質。多年來建立的專業團隊及穩定供應鏈，確保每一批產品都能達到一致的高標準。無論是經典的港式咖啡配方，還是為客戶度身訂製的混豆方案，我們都能提供專業建議及靈活服務。Our core strength lies in our own roasting facility, which gives us complete control over quality from green bean sourcing to final delivery. Our experienced team and stable supply chain, built over decades, ensure every batch meets consistently high standards. Whether it's classic Hong Kong-style coffee blends or custom formulations tailored to your needs, we provide expert advice and flexible service.

除了咖啡與茶葉，我們亦推出自家品牌淡奶系列（佳高、利美），並擔任香港及澳門歷史悠久品牌「皇冠牌」西洋菜蜜的唯一總代理，致力傳承地道的港式飲品文化。Beyond coffee and tea, we also launched our own brand evaporated milk series (Ka Kao, Lei Mei) and serve as the sole distributor for Crown Brand watercress honey in Hong Kong and Macau — a historic brand dedicated to preserving authentic Hong Kong beverage culture.

捷安咖啡不只是供應商，更是您長期可靠的合作夥伴。我們深明餐飲業的需求與挑戰，憑藉超過半世紀的經驗與誠信，與500+餐飲夥伴建立互信關係，共同成長。Tsit On Coffee is more than a supplier — we are your long-term, reliable partner. We deeply understand the needs and challenges of the food and beverage industry. With over half a century of experience and integrity, we have built trust with 500+ restaurant partners, growing together.

重要里程Key Milestones

見證香港餐飲業發展Witnessing HK F&B Evolution

1970

公司成立Founded

捷安咖啡在香港成立，開始服務本地餐飲業，提供優質咖啡及茶餐廳食材。 Tsit On Coffee established in Hong Kong, serving the local food and beverage industry with quality coffee and cha chaan teng ingredients.

1975

自家烘焙工廠Own Roastery

建立自家烘焙設施，從生豆採購到烘焙出貨全程掌控，確保品質穩定。 Established our own roasting facility with full control from green bean procurement to delivery, ensuring consistent quality.

2010

拓展產品線Expanding Portfolio

推出自家品牌淡奶系列（佳高、利美），成為皇冠牌西洋菜蜜港澳獨家代理。 Launched own brand milk series (Ka Kao, Lei Mei) and became sole HK/Macau distributor for Crown Brand watercress honey.
