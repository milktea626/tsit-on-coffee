品牌起源Our Origin

1970年，一個關於咖啡的夢想 1970 — A Dream About Coffee

捷安咖啡由創辦人在香港創立，最初以一台小型烘豆機和對香港咖啡文化的深厚熱忱起步，專注於為本地茶餐廳提供優質咖啡豆。 Tsit On Coffee was founded in Hong Kong with a small roaster and a deep passion for Hong Kong's unique coffee culture — focused from day one on supplying quality coffee to local cha chaan teng restaurants.

數十年來，我們持續擴展產品線，加入港式奶茶茶葉調配及茶餐廳乾糧批發，並建立了自家物流車隊，服務範圍由香港擴展至全球各地。 Over the decades we expanded to include HK milk tea blending and cha chaan teng dry goods, and built our own logistics fleet — growing from a local supplier to an international wholesale partner.

時至今日，捷安咖啡已成為超過500間香港餐廳的首選批發夥伴，我們的咖啡豆及奶茶茶葉更出口至世界各地。 Today, Tsit On Coffee is the preferred wholesale partner for over 500 Hong Kong restaurants, with coffee beans and tea blends exported worldwide.

發展歷程Our Journey

55年的香港咖啡傳承55 Years of Hong Kong Coffee Heritage

1970

捷安咖啡正式創立Tsit On Coffee Founded

創辦人在香港成立捷安咖啡，購置首台烘豆機，開始為本地茶餐廳供應新鮮烘焙咖啡豆。Founded with a single roasting machine in Hong Kong, supplying freshly roasted coffee beans to local cha chaan teng restaurants.

1978

擴展港式奶茶茶葉業務Expansion into HK Milk Tea Blends

推出首款港式奶茶茶葉配方，以多種錫蘭茶葉調配，迅速成為茶餐廳最受歡迎的選擇之一。Launched our first HK milk tea blend using multiple Ceylon tea varieties — quickly becoming a favourite among cha chaan teng owners.

1985

建立自家烘焙工廠Own Roasting Factory Established

投資建立全新烘焙工廠，引進先進烘焙設備，生產能力大幅提升，可為更多餐廳穩定供貨。Invested in a purpose-built roasting factory with advanced equipment, significantly increasing capacity to serve a growing restaurant base.

1995

開展乾糧及罐頭批發業務Dry & Canned Goods Wholesale Launched

回應茶餐廳客戶需求，增設乾糧及罐頭食品批發服務，成為業界首家提供一站式茶餐廳食材供應的批發商。Responding to customer demand, launched dry and canned goods wholesale — becoming one of the first one-stop cha chaan teng supply wholesalers in HK.

2005

建立自家物流車隊Own Logistics Fleet Established

建立捷安專屬物流車隊，覆蓋全港18區，實現穩定定期送貨，大幅提升客戶服務質素。Built Tsit On's own logistics fleet covering all 18 Hong Kong districts, enabling reliable scheduled deliveries and significantly improving service quality.

2012

開展海外郵寄服務International Shipping Service Launched

正式推出海外郵寄服務，讓海外港人及香港咖啡愛好者可隨時訂購正宗港式咖啡及奶茶茶葉。Launched international shipping, enabling Hong Kong diaspora and global coffee lovers to order authentic HK coffee and milk tea blends worldwide.

2020

訂製混豆服務全面升級Custom Blending Service Enhanced

推出全面升級的訂製混豆服務，為餐廳提供個人化的咖啡及奶茶茶葉配方，助客戶打造獨一無二的飲品特色。Launched an enhanced custom blending service helping restaurants create signature coffee and tea blends that set them apart from competitors.

2025

超過半世紀，持續前行55+ Years and Still Growing

捷安咖啡繼續以創始人的精神前行，為超過500間餐廳合作夥伴提供最優質的咖啡批發服務。Tsit On Coffee continues forward with the founder's spirit, serving 500+ restaurant partners with the same passion and quality commitment since 1970.
