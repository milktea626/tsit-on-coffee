服務流程Our Process

四個步驟，打造您的專屬配方Four Steps to Your Exclusive Blend

1

免費諮詢及口味溝通Free Consultation & Flavour Discussion

與我們的調配師會面，分享您對咖啡或奶茶口味的要求，包括濃淡、酸甜苦澀的平衡，以及您目標客群的口味偏好。Meet with our blenders to share your vision — strength, balance of acidity/sweetness/bitterness, and your target customers' preferences.

2

樣品調製及品嚐Sample Blending & Tasting

我們的調配師根據您的要求，調製多款樣品供您試飲比較，並根據您的反饋進一步調整配方。Our blenders create multiple sample blends based on your brief. You taste, compare and give feedback for further refinement.

3

配方確認及批量試產Formula Confirmation & Trial Production

配方確認後，進行批量試產，再次確認品質符合您的要求，並簽署配方保密協議。After formula approval, a trial production run confirms quality meets your requirements. A confidentiality agreement is signed.

4

定期生產及穩定供應Regular Production & Stable Supply

您的專屬配方納入我們的定期生產排程，確保穩定、持續地供應您所需的批量，配合您的用量靈活調整。Your exclusive blend enters our regular production schedule — stable, continuous supply at your required volume, flexible to your needs.
