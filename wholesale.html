常見問題FAQ

批發合作常見問題Wholesale FAQ

批發的最低訂購量是多少？What is the minimum order quantity?

咖啡豆最低訂量為5kg，港式奶茶茶葉最低10kg，乾糧及罐頭可按產品個別訂購，不設整體最低金額限制。Minimum 5kg for coffee beans, 10kg for HK milk tea blends, and dry/canned goods can be ordered per product with no minimum total order value.

如何取得批發價格目錄？How do I get the wholesale price list?

提交批發申請後，我們的銷售代表會與您聯絡，提供完整的批發價格目錄及條款詳情。After submitting your application, our sales representative will contact you with the complete wholesale price list and terms.

可以先試用樣品再決定嗎？Can I try samples before committing?

可以。我們提供免費樣品試用服務，讓您的廚師和咖啡師在正式訂購前先評估我們的產品品質。Yes. We offer free samples so your chefs and baristas can evaluate our products before committing to regular orders.

付款方式及條款如何？What are the payment methods and terms?

接受銀行轉賬、支票及現金付款。長期合作夥伴可申請月結30天付款安排。We accept bank transfer, cheque and cash. Established long-term partners can apply for 30-day monthly billing terms.
