捷安咖啡訂製混豆服務全面升級 Tsit On Coffee Launches Enhanced Custom Blending Service

捷安咖啡 · 2025年1月 · 4分鐘閱讀Tsit On Coffee · January 2025 · 4 min read

首頁Home› 最新消息News› 訂製混豆升級Custom Blending Upgrade

自2025年1月起，捷安咖啡全面升級訂製混豆服務，為香港餐飲業提供更靈活、更快捷、更多元化的訂製咖啡及奶茶茶葉配方解決方案。

升級內容一覽

📦 更靈活的最低訂量
新服務將訂製混豆的最低起訂量大幅降低，讓中小型茶餐廳及咖啡廳也能負擔得起獨家配方，不再是大型連鎖店的專利。
⚡ 更快的樣品製作週期
全新樣品製作流程將樣品交付時間縮短至5–7個工作天，讓您更快試用、確認及正式落單。
🌍 更多產地選擇
新增多個精品咖啡產地庫存，包括衣索比亞、哥倫比亞、瓜地馬拉等單品原料，可靈活加入訂製配方。
📋 專業配方記錄
每個訂製配方均有書面記錄存檔，確保每次訂單都能完美重現，維持一致的品質和口味。

了解訂製混豆服務詳情

From January 2025, Tsit On Coffee has launched a comprehensive upgrade to its custom blending service — delivering more flexible minimum orders, faster sample turnaround and an expanded range of single-origin options for Hong Kong's F&B industry.

What's New

📦 More Flexible Minimum Orders
We've significantly reduced the minimum order quantity for custom blends — making exclusive coffee formulas accessible to independent restaurants and small cafés, not just large chains.
⚡ Faster Sample Turnaround
Our new sample production process delivers samples within 5–7 working days — so you can taste, refine and confirm your blend faster than ever before.
🌍 Expanded Origin Selection
We've added new single-origin stocks including Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala — giving blenders more building blocks to craft the precise flavour profile you want.
📋 Documented Formula Records
Every custom blend is recorded and archived, ensuring perfect reproducibility on every order — consistent quality, every time.
