新品：埃塞俄比亞耶加雪夫季節性單品咖啡豆 New Arrival: Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Seasonal Single Origin

捷安咖啡 · 2024年12月 · 4分鐘閱讀Tsit On Coffee · December 2024 · 4 min read

首頁Home› 最新消息News› 耶加雪夫新品Yirgacheffe Arrival

捷安咖啡現已引入全新季節性單品咖啡豆——埃塞俄比亞耶加雪夫（Ethiopia Yirgacheffe），以其獨特的花香及果酸特質著稱，適合精品咖啡廳及追求高品質的餐廳批發採購。

關於耶加雪夫產區

耶加雪夫（Yirgacheffe）位於埃塞俄比亞南部，是全球最著名的精品咖啡產區之一。這裡高海拔、涼爽的氣候及肥沃的土壤，孕育出帶有明顯花香及柑橘酸的咖啡豆，被譽為「精品咖啡皇后」。

捷安咖啡今次引入的耶加雪夫批次，採用日曬處理法（Natural Process），進一步提升了咖啡的果香濃度及甜感，帶來更豐富的風味層次。

耶加雪夫的花香果酸風味，特別適合：

  • 提供精品單品咖啡的咖啡廳

  • 想為訂製配方加入花果香元素的業者

Tsit On Coffee is pleased to introduce a new seasonal single-origin coffee: Ethiopia Yirgacheffe — celebrated for its distinctive floral and citrus character, and now available for wholesale to Hong Kong specialty coffee shops and quality-focused restaurants.

About the Yirgacheffe Region

Yirgacheffe, in southern Ethiopia, is one of the world's most celebrated specialty coffee origins. Its high altitude, cool climate and rich soils produce coffees with a remarkably pronounced floral aroma and bright citrus acidity — qualities that have earned it the title of "Queen of Specialty Coffee."

This particular lot is processed using the Natural (dry) method, which amplifies the fruit-forward character and sweetness, delivering additional layers of complexity to an already exceptional coffee.

Flavour Profile

🌸
Floral Jasmine, rose, light blossom
🍋
Citrus Acidity Lemon, bergamot, bright acidity
🍑
Fruit Notes Peach, blueberry, sweet fruit
🍵
Texture Light, delicate, clean finish

OriginEthiopia, Yirgacheffe

Best Brew MethodsPour-over, Cold Brew, Espresso

Who Is This For?

The Yirgacheffe's floral and fruit-forward profile is particularly suited to:

  • Specialty coffee shops offering single-origin pour-over menus
  • Restaurants seeking a distinctive, premium coffee experience
  • Hotels and Western restaurants looking for seasonal menu features
  • Operators wanting to add floral-fruit notes to a custom blend
