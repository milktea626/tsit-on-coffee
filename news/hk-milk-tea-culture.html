港式絲襪奶茶：一杯凝聚半世紀的飲品文化 HK Silk Stocking Milk Tea: A Beverage Culture Five Decades in the Making

捷安咖啡 · 2025年2月 · 6分鐘閱讀Tsit On Coffee · February 2025 · 6 min read

首頁Home› 最新消息News› 港式奶茶文化HK Milk Tea Culture

港式奶茶，俗稱「絲襪奶茶」，早已超越一杯普通飲品的範疇，成為香港飲食文化不可分割的象徵。2014年，港式奶茶製作技藝正式被列入香港非物質文化遺產名錄，標誌著這杯飲品在香港文化中的重要地位。

一、港式奶茶的起源

港式奶茶的歷史可追溯至20世紀初。英國殖民時期，下午茶文化傳入香港，但昂貴的英式奶茶並非普通市民能負擔。精明的香港茶餐廳老闆便將錫蘭紅茶與淡奶（花奶）混合，創造出更濃郁、更實惠的本地版本——港式奶茶就此誕生。

「絲襪奶茶」這個名稱來自製作工藝：茶師以一個形似絲襪的長布茶袋過濾茶葉，使茶湯更加幼滑細膩，口感有如「絲般滑順」，因而得名。

二、製作工藝的精髓

🍃 茶葉選配
正宗港式奶茶採用多種錫蘭紅茶拼配而成，通常包括肯尼亞、斯里蘭卡等不同產地的茶葉，各司其職——有的提供濃郁茶色，有的帶來清香茶味，有的賦予獨特的澀感。捷安咖啡的奶茶茶葉即是根據這一傳統調配。
⏱️ 浸泡時間
茶葉需以高溫熱水沖泡，浸泡時間控制在特定範圍內，確保茶湯濃度適中，不苦不澀，茶香充分釋放。
🥛 撞茶技藝（「撞奶」）
最具特色的環節是「撞奶」：將熱茶與淡奶在高處反覆撞入容器，利用衝擊力使兩者充分融合，並製造出細膩的奶泡，令奶茶口感更加絲滑。
🧦 絲布過濾
透過長布茶袋反覆過濾，去除所有茶渣，使茶湯質感更加幼滑均勻，這也是「絲襪奶茶」得名的由來。

三、非物質文化遺產的意義

2014年，港式奶茶製作技藝獲列入香港非物質文化遺產代表作名錄。這不僅是對這門傳統工藝的認可，更提醒我們香港獨特的飲食文化需要保護和傳承。

作為本地咖啡和奶茶茶葉的批發商，捷安咖啡深感責任重大。我們致力於向茶餐廳提供正宗的奶茶茶葉配方，讓每一杯港式奶茶都保留那份獨特的「香港味道」。

Hong Kong milk tea — affectionately known as "silk stocking milk tea" — has long transcended the status of a simple beverage. In 2014, the art of making Hong Kong-style milk tea was formally inscribed on the Hong Kong Intangible Cultural Heritage list, cementing its place as an irreplaceable part of the city's food and cultural identity.

1. The Origins of Hong Kong Milk Tea

The story of HK milk tea begins in the early 20th century. During the British colonial era, afternoon tea culture arrived in Hong Kong — but authentic English tea was too expensive for ordinary residents. Resourceful cha chaan teng owners began blending Ceylon black tea with evaporated milk (花奶, "faa naai"), creating a richer, more affordable local version. Hong Kong milk tea was born.

The "silk stocking" name comes from the technique: tea masters strain the brew through a long cloth straining bag that resembles a silk stocking, producing an exceptionally smooth, velvety texture. The name stuck, and so did the tradition.

2. The Craft Behind Every Cup

🍃 Tea Leaf Blending
Authentic HK milk tea uses a multi-origin Ceylon tea blend — typically combining leaves from Kenya, Sri Lanka and other origins. Each plays a role: some provide deep colour and body, others bring bright aroma, and others contribute that characteristic astringency. Tsit On Coffee's milk tea blends follow this same tradition.
⏱️ Steep Time Control
Tea leaves are brewed at high temperature and steeped for a precise duration — long enough for full flavour extraction, but controlled to avoid bitterness. This balance is the mark of a skilled tea master.
🥛 The "Pour & Crash" Technique
The most distinctive step: hot brewed tea is repeatedly poured from a height into evaporated milk and back, creating a rapid collision that fully emulsifies the two — producing a silky micro-foam texture unique to HK milk tea.
🧦 Cloth Straining
The brew is passed repeatedly through a long cloth straining bag, removing all tea particles and producing an impossibly smooth, uniform texture — the technique that earned the drink its "silk stocking" nickname.

3. Why Intangible Cultural Heritage Status Matters

The 2014 inscription on Hong Kong's Intangible Cultural Heritage list was more than symbolic recognition — it was a call to preserve and pass on a dying craft. As younger generations move away from traditional cha chaan teng dining, there is a real risk that the precise skills and tea blending knowledge built over decades could be lost.
