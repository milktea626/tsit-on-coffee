捷安咖啡烘焙工廠Tsit On Coffee Roastery

自1985年設立自家烘焙工廠，從生豆選購到烘焙出品，每一步都在我們的掌控之中。Our own roasting factory since 1985 — from green bean sourcing to finished product, every step is under our control.

品質從源頭開始Quality Starts at the Source

為何自家烘焙如此重要？Why Does Own Roasting Matter?

坐擁自家烘焙工廠，讓捷安咖啡能夠完全掌控每批咖啡豆的品質——從生豆的篩選、烘焙曲線的設定，到出廠前的嚴格品質測試。 Having our own roasting factory means Tsit On Coffee has complete control over every batch — from green bean selection and roast profile to quality testing before dispatch.

不同於依賴第三方烘焙的批發商，我們能夠確保每批咖啡豆的新鮮度，根據季節調整烘焙曲線，並為客戶提供訂製烘焙服務。 Unlike wholesalers relying on third-party roasters, we guarantee freshness of every batch, adjust roast profiles seasonally, and offer custom roasting for clients.

烘焙流程Our Process

從生豆到杯中的六個步驟Six Steps from Green Bean to Cup

1

生豆採購及篩選Green Bean Sourcing & Sorting

我們的採購團隊直接與全球30+個產地的莊園合作，嚴格篩選每批生豆，確保只有最優質的生豆進入我們的工廠。Our procurement team works directly with estates across 30+ origins worldwide, rigorously sorting every batch to ensure only the finest green beans enter our factory.

2

樣品試烘及杯測Sample Roasting & Cupping

每批新生豆到廠後，首先進行小批量試烘，由我們的Q Grader杯測師進行嚴格杯測評估，確認達標後才進行大批量烘焙。Each new batch undergoes small-scale trial roasting first, evaluated by our Q Grader cupping specialists before large-scale production is approved.

3

精準烘焙曲線設定Precision Roast Profile Setting

我們為每款咖啡豆設計獨特的烘焙曲線，控制溫度、時間及氣流，將每種咖啡豆的最佳風味完美呈現。We design a unique roast profile for each coffee variety, controlling temperature, time and airflow to bring out the best flavours of every bean.

4

烘焙及冷卻Roasting & Cooling

使用業界頂級烘豆機，按預設曲線精準烘焙。烘焙完成後立即快速冷卻，鎖住咖啡豆的最佳風味。Using top-grade industrial roasters, we follow the preset profile precisely. Immediately after roasting, rapid cooling locks in the optimal flavour.

5

品質控制及杯測Quality Control & Final Cupping

每批烘焙完成後均進行最終杯測，確保品質達到我們的嚴格標準。不合格的批次不會出廠。Every batch undergoes a final cupping after roasting. Any batch not meeting our strict standards is not dispatched.

6

包裝及出貨Packaging & Dispatch

通過品質測試後，咖啡豆在靜置排氣後即時包裝，確保新鮮抵達您的餐廳。After quality approval, beans are packaged with one-way degassing valves after a brief rest period — ensuring freshness upon arrival at your restaurant.

設備及產能Equipment & Capacity

專業設備，保障穩定供應Professional Equipment for Consistent Supply

⚙️

工業級烘豆機Industrial Roasters

配備多台業界頂級工業烘豆機，每日可烘焙大量咖啡豆，穩定供應香港各大餐廳所需。Multiple top-grade industrial roasters enabling high daily capacity — stable supply for all our Hong Kong restaurant partners.

🖥️

數位化烘焙監控Digital Roast Monitoring

採用先進的數位化烘焙監控系統，實時記錄每批次的烘焙曲線數據，確保每批出品的一致性。Advanced digital monitoring systems record every batch's roast profile in real-time, ensuring consistency batch after batch.

🌡️

溫控倉儲系統Temperature-Controlled Storage

烘焙完成的咖啡豆存放於恆溫恆濕倉庫，最大限度地保持咖啡豆的新鮮度和香氣。Finished beans are stored in temperature and humidity-controlled warehouses to maximise freshness and aroma retention.
